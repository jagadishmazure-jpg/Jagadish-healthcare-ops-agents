# SOP-01: Triage and ED Boarding (Lakeside General Hospital)

Operational policy for emergency department intake flow. Clinical triage
decisions belong to licensed staff; this document governs the operational
handling of arrivals once triaged.

## Acuity bands

Triage assigns every arrival an acuity band from 1 (most urgent) to 5 (least
urgent), following the standard five-level emergency severity index used
across the industry.

- **Acuity 1-2:** resuscitation / emergent. Reassess at least every 15 minutes
  while in the ED. These patients are never assigned to a general ward bed;
  acuity 1 requires an ICU bed, acuity 2 an ICU or stepdown bed.
- **Acuity 3:** urgent. Reassess hourly. Eligible for stepdown, medical, or
  surgical beds depending on the presenting category.
- **Acuity 4-5:** less urgent / non-urgent. Reassess every two hours. Eligible
  for medical, surgical, or pediatric beds.

## Boarding definition

A patient is *boarding* when the decision to admit has been made but no
inpatient bed is yet available. Boarding time is measured from the admit
decision to bed assignment.

## Boarding thresholds

- Any acuity-1 boarder waiting more than 8 hours triggers a **T4 escalation**.
- More than 10 total boarders triggers a **T3 escalation**.
- The bed manager runs the acuity-weighted bed match at least every 2 hours
  while any boarder is waiting.
