# SOP-03: Discharge Planning (Lakeside General Hospital)

## Discharge-readiness checklist

A patient is discharge-ready only when every item passes:

1. `meds_reconciled` -- home medication list reconciled by pharmacy.
2. `followup_scheduled` -- follow-up appointment booked before discharge.
3. `discharge_summary_signed` -- attending has signed the summary.
4. `transport_arranged` -- ride or medical transport confirmed.
5. `home_care_confirmed` -- home-care services confirmed where ordered.

## Blocked discharges

Any stay with a non-passing item is *blocked*. The discharge lounge reviews
blocked stays twice daily and names the single missing item per stay so the
responsible team can clear it.

## Override policy

Discharging despite a missing item is an override. Overrides always require:

- A named approver (attending or house supervisor),
- A written reason,
- A human confirmation-gate approval (every override is gated, no threshold),
- An audit entry naming approver, reason, and the missing items.

Overrides are reviewed weekly; more than 3 overrides per ward per week
triggers a process review.
